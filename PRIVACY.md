# Privacy Policy — 5 Persona Advisory Board

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Publisher: Bert Van Dessel / BVD AI CORE
Support and privacy requests: https://github.com/harryvondiesel-web/5-persona-advisory-board/issues

## Scope

This policy covers the 5 Persona Advisory Board skills-only plugin and its
GitHub support channel. It does not replace the policies of ChatGPT, Codex,
or GitHub.

## Conversation data and purpose

The skill uses the decision, context, constraints, evidence, and answers you
provide in your conversation to prepare a decision brief and next-action plan.
Information you choose to include may contain personal or business details.
Provide only what is needed; use anonymized summaries instead of identifying
other people, customers, or confidential records.

ChatGPT or Codex processes the conversation under the host's own policies and
account settings. The publisher does not receive your conversation through
this plugin. The package contains instructions and an icon, with no MCP server,
developer API endpoint, account connection, telemetry, advertising tracker,
or credential collection.

The publisher therefore collects no chat content, chat-derived personal data,
device identifiers, location data, usage analytics, payment details, or account
credentials through the plugin itself. The plugin does not send conversation
content to the publisher, GitHub, or another developer service.

## GitHub support data and purpose

Support for this plugin is provided through GitHub issues only. No private
email support or separate support database is provided for this plugin.

If you voluntarily open or comment on a GitHub issue, your GitHub username,
public profile information, issue content, and attachments may be visible.
The maintainer uses that information to answer the request, investigate
problems, and maintain the project. Do not post chat transcripts, secrets,
sensitive records, or identifying customer information. A sanitized summary
is sufficient for support.

The publisher does not sell support information or use it for advertising.
There are no plugin-specific tracking cookies or analytics. GitHub's website
may use cookies and process visitor information under GitHub's own policy.

## Recipients

- Conversations are handled by the ChatGPT or Codex host and its service
  providers under the host's policy. The publisher has no plugin service that
  receives them.
- GitHub issues and attachments are handled by GitHub, accessible to the
  maintainer, and visible to anyone who can view this public repository.
  Others may copy publicly posted information.
- The publisher does not forward plugin conversations or issue information
  to advertising or analytics services through this plugin.

## Retention

- Plugin conversations: the publisher creates no conversation copies or logs
  through this plugin, so publisher-side retention is zero. The host's own
  retention rules and your account settings still apply.
- GitHub support content: retained on the public repository indefinitely
  unless removed through GitHub. GitHub may retain edit history, backups, or
  other records under its own policies. The publisher does not control
  GitHub's retention timelines. No separate publisher support database or
  automatic issue-data export is operated for this plugin.

## Your controls

You can omit or anonymize personal information, stop using the skill, and
disable or uninstall the plugin. Manage your chats, data controls, and deletion
requests through ChatGPT or Codex; the publisher cannot delete host-held chats.

Use GitHub's controls to edit your own issue content. For correction, access,
or removal requests concerning information the maintainer can control, use
this project's issue tracker or GitHub support. Identify the issue or comment
without repeating private information in a public request. Removal cannot
guarantee deletion of copies already made by others, and GitHub controls its
own account records and platform retention.

## Changes and contact

Material changes will be published in this repository with an updated effective
date. A future version introducing a hosted service, telemetry, or account
connections requires updated disclosures before collection begins.

For plugin support or privacy questions, use:
https://github.com/harryvondiesel-web/5-persona-advisory-board/issues

Do not post credentials, identity documents, payment information, health
records, or other sensitive information in the issue tracker.
